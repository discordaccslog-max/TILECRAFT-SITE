About

What this is, and why it is built the way it is.

What Tilecraft Digital makes

Tilecraft Digital makes original seamless patterns and textures, and the tools to work with them. They are sold as Collections — fixed sets of repeats around one idea — and as Studio Editions, which are tools for making and exporting patterns rather than sets of them.

Everything is delivered through the Studio, a workspace that runs in a web browser. You open a product there, change its colors and scale, and export what you need.

Why patterns at all

A seamless repeat is a small piece of geometry that has to be exactly right. The edges have to meet, the corners have to resolve, and the eye has to not find the grid. Getting that wrong is easy and getting it right is tedious, which is why so many patterns sold as seamless are not quite seamless, and why so many that are seamless look like a tiled wall.

Everything here is built so that the repeat is correct by construction. The tiles are generated in code, so the edges are not stitched together after the fact — they are the same edge. That is a dull-sounding property that matters enormously the moment you scale a pattern up across a length of fabric.

Why it runs in your browser

The Studio could have been built as a service with your work on a server. It is not, and that was a deliberate choice.

Your work stays on your device. Patterns you save, colorways you build, and images you bring in are stored in your own browser and never sent anywhere. There is no account, no login, and no upload. A tool that needs your artwork does not need to take a copy of it, and the less there is on a server, the less there is to lose, leak, or hold over you.

The same choice is why the Studio works offline once it has loaded, and why your license is a code you hold rather than a row in our database. The honest cost of all this is that we cannot recover anything for you if your browser data is cleared — so the Studio can export a backup of everything at once, and you should take one.

How the patterns are made

The patterns are created with AI assistance and generated in code. They are not made with AI image generators, and they are not made by using or tracing anyone else’s artwork.

In practice that means the shapes, spacing, and repeat geometry are written as code, and AI was used as assistance while writing and refining that code. The output is drawn by the code, mathematically, which is also why every repeat tiles exactly.

Every pattern is then checked by the build that produces it. Each one is rendered and its opposite edges are compared pixel by pixel, so a repeat that did not actually tile could not ship; anything that failed was redrawn rather than shipped. The tiled result was also reviewed by eye at several scales.

How this site is built

It is static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no third-party scripts of any kind. Every font and every library is served from this site rather than from somewhere else, so loading a page here does not tell anybody else that you were reading it.

The compositions you see across the site are drawn in code, in the browser, as decoration. They are illustrations, not previews of the patterns you would receive, and the site says so wherever one appears.

What you can expect

Plain descriptions of what each product includes and does, a price that does not move around, a commercial license written in language you can actually read, and a refund within 14 days if you want one, no reason required.

You will not find countdown timers, fake discounts, invented review counts, or claims about what these patterns will do for your business. We do not know what they will do for your business.
