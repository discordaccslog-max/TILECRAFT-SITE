About your cart

Your cart is kept in this browser and nowhere else. It is not sent anywhere, it is not tied to an account, and we cannot see it. Clearing your browsing data clears it, and it does not follow you to another device.

Prices are read from the catalog each time this page is drawn, so a cart left here for a while always shows what its contents cost today rather than what they cost when they were added.
